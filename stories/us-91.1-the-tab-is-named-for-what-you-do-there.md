# US-91.1 — The tab is named for what you do there

**Status:** Testing
**Phase:** 91 — Usability: the dashboard reads like the job
**Depends on:** nothing (the rest of Phase 91 builds on this tab)

## Story

As the manager, the first tab of Things to Do is called **Waiting on you**.
That names my *state* — blocked — not the *act* the tab exists for. Every
row on it ends in one of two clicks: dispatch it, or approve it and let it
dispatch. The tab is where work leaves my hands and enters the factory.

Naming it **Dispatch** says that, and it sets up the rest of this phase: a
tab called Dispatch can honestly carry a section showing what is already
being dispatched (us-91.2) without reading as a contradiction.

Nothing about what the tab *contains* changes here. This is the rename, on
its own, so the diff that follows it is about behaviour.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC1 — the tab reads "Dispatch".** The first trigger in
  [dashboard-tabs.tsx](../apps/web/src/app/(app)/dashboard/dashboard-tabs.tsx)
  is labelled `Dispatch`. Its icon and its count badge are unchanged — the
  badge still shows `waitingCount`, which is the number of groups' items,
  not a re-derived number (us-87.2: a badge is a count).
- **AC2 — the URL key does not change.** The tab still rides `?tab=` with
  the key `waiting`, and it is still the default (no param). A link someone
  saved or an agent wrote (`/dashboard?tab=factory`) resolves exactly as
  before. Renaming the key would break saved links to buy nothing.
- **AC3 — no user-visible string on the dashboard still says "Waiting on
  you".** That includes the empty state, any heading, and the aria/title
  attributes on the tab. Comments in the source may keep the old name where
  they are quoting history.
- **AC4 — the other three tabs are untouched.** In the factory, Completed
  and Releases keep their labels, order, icons and counts.

## Out of scope

- The `waiting` **stage** label in
  [stage-state.tsx](../apps/web/src/components/stage-state.tsx) — that is
  a work-item stage ("this item is at a manager gate"), a different noun
  from the tab, and it is rendered on the work-item page and elsewhere.
  Leave it reading "Waiting on you".
- The wording on the work-item page's live activity line ("Waiting on your
  answer — see Things to Do") — it points at the *page*, not the tab.
- Any change to which items appear on the tab, or to their grouping — that
  is us-91.2 and us-91.4.

## Verified (2026-08-14, localhost against build-mill-dev)

- The first tab reads **Dispatch**; icon and count badge unchanged (`Dispatch 11`).
- `?tab=completed` and `?tab=factory` still resolve — the URL key stayed `waiting`,
  so saved links are intact.
- No user-visible "Waiting on you" left on the dashboard. The `waiting` **stage**
  label and the work-item page's "see Things to Do" line were left alone, as
  scoped.
