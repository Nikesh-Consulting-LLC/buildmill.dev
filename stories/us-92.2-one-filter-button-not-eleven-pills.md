# US-92.2 — One filter button, not eleven pills

**Status:** Testing
**Phase:** 92 — The app on a phone
**Depends on:** [us-91.5](us-91.5-the-status-filter-is-a-set-not-a-choice.md) (the status set this puts behind the button)

## Story

As the manager, Work Items on a phone opens with **eleven controls** before a
single work item. Counted at 375px: the project filter, `All types`, Feature,
Bug, Chore, Story, the status set, and the three lens switches (Outline,
Board, Table). They wrap to four rows and push the list below the fold — on
the page whose entire job is showing me the list.

Then the lenses themselves: **Table is `min-w-[52rem]`** — 856px of nine
columns in a 375px viewport, so it scrolls sideways inside its own box.
**Board** is five phase columns, which is a desktop idea by construction. On
a phone there is one useful lens, and it is Outline.

A phone is not a small desktop. Filters are something you *open*, adjust and
close — not furniture you keep on screen.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC1 — one button below `md`.** The toolbar collapses to a single
  **Filters** button that opens a sheet. The button carries a count of active
  filters (`Filters · 2`) so the manager can see the list is narrowed without
  opening anything — the trigger must never be silent about that, for the
  same reason us-91.5's pill says what it hides.
- **AC2 — the sheet holds all of them**, grouped and full-width: project,
  type, status (the us-91.5 checkbox set), with `Apply` and a `Reset` that
  returns to the default (merged and done unchecked). Choices persist as they
  do today.
- **AC3 — search stays outside the sheet.** Finding a known item by name is
  the most common phone task on this page and must not cost two taps.
- **AC4 — Outline is the phone lens.** Below `md` the lens switch offers
  Outline and Table only; Board is hidden, not broken — five columns cannot
  be made to work at 375px, and pretending otherwise wastes the manager's
  time. At `md`+ all three remain.
- **AC5 — the Table lens is honest about being wide.** If it is kept below
  `md`, it drops to the columns that fit (id, title, status) rather than
  scrolling 856px sideways. Whatever is dropped is on the row's second line.
- **AC6 — an outline row is thumb-sized.** Title on its own line, then a meta
  line (type, status, cost); the whole row is the tap target to open the item.
- **AC7 — the counts stay truthful.** `N of M`, the epic counts and the
  project counts describe the same sets they do on desktop.

## Out of scope

- Changing what the filters filter, or adding new ones.
- The abandoned-items view.
- Bulk selection on mobile — it is a desktop affordance and stays one.

## Verified (2026-08-14, localhost at 375x812)

- **Eleven controls became three plus a button**: the project filter, Outline
  and Table, and **`Filters 1`** — the count so the trigger is never silent
  about a narrowed list.
- **Board is hidden below `md`** and returns at `md`+.
- **The Table lens went from 856px to 353px** and now *fits* the 375px
  viewport with no scroll of any kind: ID · Title · Status · Cost. Type, Epic,
  Project and Updated drop below `md`, and the bulk-select column goes with
  them — its bar is desktop-only, so it was costing a phone 32px for nothing.
