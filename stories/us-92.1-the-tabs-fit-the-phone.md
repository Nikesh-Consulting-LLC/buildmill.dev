# US-92.1 — Things to Do fits in a hand

**Status:** Completed
**Phase:** 92 — The app on a phone
**Depends on:** [us-91.2](us-91.2-dispatch-opens-with-what-is-already-running.md) (In Progress is one of the sections this makes mobile)

## Story

As the manager, Things to Do is the page I open on a phone — it is the one
that tells me whether anything needs me. It is also the page that survives
the phone worst.

**The tabs do not fit.** Measured at 375px: Dispatch 129 + In the factory 159
+ Completed 143 + Releases 124 = **555px of tabs in a 375px bar**. The bar is
`overflow-x-auto`, so Completed and Releases exist but are off-screen behind
a scroll gesture with no scrollbar and no affordance. Two of the four tabs
are, in practice, hidden.

**In Progress has no mobile form.** us-91.2 shipped it as a `<table>` with no
card variant, while the list below it has had one since us-68.4. So the newest
section on the tab is the one that breaks the layout it sits in.

**And the page's preamble is long.** Above the tabs sit the stalled-queue
banner, incidents, clarifications, parked runs and exhausted budgets. Each
earns its place on a desktop; stacked on a phone they push the actual work
below two screens of scrolling.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC1 — every tab is reachable without a horizontal gesture.** Below `md`
  the four tabs become a control that fits: a full-width **select** naming
  the current facet and its count (`Dispatch · 11`), or a 2×2 grid of
  segments. Whichever is chosen, all four are visible or one tap away, and
  the counts survive — they are why the manager looks.
- **AC2 — the choice still rides the URL.** `?tab=` behaves exactly as it
  does on desktop, so a link shared from a phone opens the same facet on a
  laptop.
- **AC3 — In Progress becomes cards below `md`.** One card per running item:
  type badge and id, title, agent, elapsed, heard, and the CLI-window button
  at a real tap size. The table stays at `md`+ unchanged. It follows the
  pattern us-68.4 already set for the list below it — a card per item, one
  primary action, no squeezed columns.
- **AC4 — the phone's first screen is work, not warnings.** Below `md` the
  preamble cards (incidents, clarifications, parked runs, exhausted budgets,
  stalled queue) collapse into **one summary strip** — a count per kind, e.g.
  `2 incidents · 1 question · 1 over budget` — that expands on tap. Nothing
  is removed; it stops costing two screens before the first row.
- **AC5 — project groups stay collapsible on a phone**, since that is where
  folding matters most (us-91.4 already renders group headers in the mobile
  card list; they must fold there too).
- **AC6 — tap targets are ≥40px.** Every row action on the mobile layouts —
  dispatch, approve, requeue, CLI — meets it. Today several are `size-6`
  icon buttons inherited from the table.
- **AC7 — nothing changes at `md` and above.** This story is additive below
  the breakpoint; the desktop tables, counts and behaviour are untouched.

## Out of scope

- Changing which items appear on any tab, or their order.
- A bottom tab bar or any app-shell navigation change — [us-91.17](us-91.17-the-workspace-picker-travels.md)
  put the drawer right; this is the page inside it.
- The Releases tab's own contents (that is [us-92.3](us-92.3-a-release-is-a-card-with-its-buttons-showing.md)).

## Verified (2026-08-14, localhost at 375x812)

- **AC1/AC2**: the tab bar is `display: none` below `md`; a full-width select
  (343px) reads **`Dispatch · 11`** and lists all four with their counts.
  Choosing Completed moved the URL to `?tab=completed` and the trigger to
  `Completed · 12` — the facet still rides the URL.
- **AC3**: with a live claim (a temporary synthetic run, since removed) the
  section rendered a card — *"story US-2.1.20 · Running · Transcript copy and
  export … pod-001-5 · 22m elapsed · heard just now"* — and the table did not
  render at all below `md`. No horizontal scroll at 375px.
- **AC4**: the five preamble blocks fold behind **"1 stalled queue"**, which
  expands to the full banner (Open Workers and all). The tab control now sits
  at y=236 instead of below a screen of warnings.
- **AC6**: the card's CLI button is `h-10`; the list below it already used
  `h-11` for its primary action (us-68.4).
