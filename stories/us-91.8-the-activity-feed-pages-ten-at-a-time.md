# US-91.8 — The activity feed pages ten at a time, and asks about projects once

**Status:** Completed
**Phase:** 91 — Usability: the dashboard reads like the job
**Depends on:** nothing

## Story

As the manager, the Activity page asks me which projects I care about
**twice**: the global project filter in the page header (Phase 64 put it
there so one pick holds across every surface), and a second `Project` select
inside the feed card that only this page has. They are not even the same
kind of control — one is a multi-select that persists, the other a
single-select that resets on reload — and when they disagree the feed is
narrowed by a filter I set on a different page.

And the feed itself is one long scroll: fifty rows, then a button that adds
fifty more, up to four hundred. When I am reading what happened this morning,
I want a small window I can step through — not a page that grows under me.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC1 — the in-card Project select is gone.** The feed is scoped solely by
  the global project filter in the page header, which
  [page.tsx](../apps/web/src/app/(app)/activity/page.tsx) already applies to
  both the feed rows and the "Working now" strip before they reach the
  client. The `project` state, its `projectItems`, and its clause in
  `filtered` come out of
  [activity-feed.tsx](../apps/web/src/app/(app)/activity/activity-feed.tsx).
- **AC2 — Actor, Kind and Failures-only stay.** They filter within a
  project scope rather than duplicating it, and they keep their current
  behaviour.
- **AC3 — the row still names its project.** With the per-card filter gone,
  a row must still say which project it belongs to, so a multi-project view
  stays readable.
- **AC4 — the feed pages, ten at a time.** Ten rows per page with a
  previous/next pager reading `N–M of T` — replacing the "show 50 more"
  reveal. Under eleven rows, no pager renders.
- **AC5 — filters reset the page.** Changing Actor, Kind or Failures-only
  returns to page 1, and the card title's count (`Feed (N, M failures)`)
  describes the filtered total, never the current page.
- **AC6 — a live refresh does not move me.** The debounced realtime refetch
  keeps the manager on the page they are reading rather than snapping back
  to page 1; new rows land above and the pager count grows. (Page 1 is the
  exception: it is the newest rows, and it may re-render in place.)
- **AC7 — the project filter still holds across surfaces.** Removing the
  local control must not touch `readGlobalProjectIds` /
  `resolveGlobalSelection` — the pick made here still holds on Work Items,
  Things to Do and Testing.

## Out of scope

- Reaching events older than the newest 400. The server still loads a
  capped window; this story pages *within* it. Deep history is a follow-on
  (and pairs with us-87.8's retention work, which decides how much history
  there is to page through at all).
- Search over the feed, or a date-range filter.
- The "Working now" strip, which is not part of the feed.
- Any change to the `activity_feed` view.

## Verified (2026-08-14, localhost against build-mill-dev)

- The in-card Project select is gone; the remaining filters are Actor, Kind and
  Failures-only. Scope comes from the page header's global project filter.
- The feed pages ten at a time: **`1–10 of 400`** with Previous/Next, and the
  card title still describes the filtered total (`Feed (400, 10 failures)`).
